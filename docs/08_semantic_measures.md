## Principle

Measures should have one documented business definition and be evaluated against the grain of the model.

## Questions for every measure

- What business question does the measure answer?
- Which fact contains the required event/value?
- Does the fact grain require `SUM`, `COUNT`, `DISTINCTCOUNT` or another aggregation?
- Which dimensions are expected to filter the measure?
- What baseline result can validate it?
